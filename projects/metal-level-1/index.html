07 / Cal Poly Pomona workshop

METAL Level 1 Manufacturing Workshop

At Cal Poly Pomona’s METAL Level 1 workshop, I practiced casting, CNC machining, and steel heat treatment. Working across the three processes made it easier to see how upstream choices affect the part you get at the end.

01 /

Casting and machining

I used Fusion 360 for modeling and FLOW-3D CAST to look for potential shrinkage defects. We made parts using sand and lost-foam casting, including a small engine model. For CNC work, I generated G-code and helped machine a cast model car.

02 /

Heat treatment trade-offs

We used time–temperature–transformation diagrams to reason about how thermal history changes steel microstructure. We then secured heat-treated coupons in a vise to compare their behavior. The exercise made the trade-off tangible: hardness helps resist indentation and wear, while ductility and toughness matter when a part must deform or absorb energy before fracturing.
