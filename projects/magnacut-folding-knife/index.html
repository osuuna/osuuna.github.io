01 / Mechanical design and fabrication

MagnaCut Folding Knife

Our team designed and fabricated a back-lock folding knife for camping use. We chose CPM MagnaCut and selected a heat treatment intended to balance blade hardness with toughness.

01 /

Mechanism and fabrication

We modeled the knife in SolidWorks in both open and closed positions, then fabricated the blade and assembled the folding mechanism. We built a dedicated bevel jig to hold the blade during grinding on the belt sander.

02 /

Heat treatment and verification

We planned the heat treatment with a thermal history plot and used stainless tool wrap to limit oxidation and decarburization during hardening. The sequence included quenching, cold treatment, and two tempering cycles. After processing, three Rockwell C measurements averaged 63 HRC, within the team’s target range.
