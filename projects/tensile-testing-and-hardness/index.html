06 / Materials testing

Tensile Testing and Hardness

Our lab team compared the tensile response of PLA, aluminum, galvanized steel, and MagnaCut. Testing both filleted and square-shouldered specimens let us observe the influence of geometry alongside differences between materials.

  • Stress–strain

Geometries

01 /

Tensile response

We compared measured stress–strain curves and inspected the specimens after testing for necking and fracture location. The results showed why geometry matters when interpreting a tensile test: a break near a shoulder may tell you as much about the local stress concentration as about the material itself.

02 /

Hardness comparison

We also took Rockwell readings on forged, control, and drilled metal coupons. The original lab sheet records the readings and scales. I would verify the scale and repeatability before treating any of those values as a material property.
