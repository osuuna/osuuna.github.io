03 / High-power rocketry

L1 Rocket

I designed and 3D printed the conical nose cone, laser cut the fins, and 3D printed the motor retainer. I used OpenRocket to compare motor configurations and evaluate stability before launch.

01 /

From CAD to airframe

The final nose cone used a straight conical profile with a shoulder to locate it in the body tube. I translated the fin profile into laser-cut parts and used a printed motor retainer to secure the motor in the airframe.

02 /

Stability and simulation

I compared H-class motor configurations and checked the location of the center of pressure relative to the center of gravity. The static margin expresses that separation in body diameters, or calibers. The figures above are from the H219T-14 simulation and are not measured flight data.
