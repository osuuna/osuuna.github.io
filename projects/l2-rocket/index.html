L2 Rocket

I’m designing and building a rocket for Level 2 certification. The project extends my L1 build into recovery electronics, airframe integration, and onboard instrumentation.

  • Avionics
  • Dual deployment

01 /

Airframe layout

I’m using OpenRocket to evaluate component placement, center of gravity, and center of pressure. The current layout defines the nose cone, body tubes, couplers, bulkheads, and fin sets. The layout view shows the airframe without a selected motor.

02 /

Recovery and instrumentation

The recovery layout uses a drogue in the lower section and a main parachute under the nose, with an electronics bay for deployment control. I’m also planning a compact payload bay for a camera and an accelerometer/gyroscope to examine flight events.
