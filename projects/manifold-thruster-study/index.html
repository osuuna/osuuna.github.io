01 /

Manifold design

I’m comparing off-the-shelf and custom manifolds, with attention to pressure ratings and how much flow each branch needs when multiple valves open. One trade is whether to use a pressure relief device downstream of the regulator or rate the downstream components for exposure to full storage pressure. The final architecture and verification plan are still in development.

02 /

Nozzle sizing trade study

The propulsion team varied the exit-to-throat area ratio across several thrust targets using a modeled 20 bar nitrogen inlet and vacuum exhaust. Increasing the ratio improved predicted specific impulse, with diminishing gains as the nozzle grew wider and longer. Comparing a conical profile with a shorter bell profile helped put those efficiency gains in the context of available space.

03 /

What the simulation shows

The team used SolidWorks Flow Simulation on a conical nozzle candidate labeled for a 75 mN design target. The model applied mass flow at the inlet and an environment pressure condition at the exit. The Mach contour predicts acceleration through the throat and supersonic flow downstream. This is a team simulation; the design target is not a measured thrust result.
