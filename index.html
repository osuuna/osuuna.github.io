02 About

Hi, I’m Ethan.

I’m Ethan Osuna, a third-year aerospace engineering transfer student at UC Irvine. As a makerspace staff member, I help students develop their projects, think through engineering decisions, and troubleshoot fabrication and electronics. I enjoy helping others understand why a design choice makes sense and giving them the confidence to keep building.

Propulsion is the field I want to focus on within aerospace. I love seeing how thermodynamics, fluid mechanics, and hardware design come together to make a propulsion system work. My cold gas propulsion work and rocketry projects have strengthened that interest, and I’m looking for opportunities to gain more experience in propulsion design, analysis, and testing.
